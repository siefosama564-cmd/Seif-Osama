About Me

Designer with a
Technical Edge

I'm Seif Osama — a graphic designer and Computer Science & Statistics student at Helwan University, graduating July 2026. Where most creatives approach design as pure art, I treat it as a system: structured, precise, measurable.

My work spans brand identity, packaging, logo design and 3D product visualization. Trained through Egypt's Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI), I've mastered the full Adobe suite — turning concepts into visual narratives that resonate and convert.

My CS background means I read data, think algorithmically, and approach problems with rigor — a rare combination in the creative world. I don't just design beautifully. I design intelligently.
